What it keeps track of

Big buttons, plain words, and nothing you don't need.

Visits and who owes you
Log a visit in a few big taps: who, how long, how they paid. Cash, check, Venmo, prepaid hours, tips and flat-rate clients all fit.
Miles, without trying
Turn on automatic mileage and your drives are recorded. Drives to and from clients count as business on their own.
Receipts
Take a photo and the amount, store and date fill in. The photo is kept with the expense.
Your schedule
One-off and repeating visits, a reminder the evening before, and a nudge to log each visit when it's done.
Invoices and statements
Text or email a receipt, an invoice or a monthly statement with the running balance.
Numbers for tax time
A one-page year-end summary and spreadsheet files your accountant can open, income split by state.
